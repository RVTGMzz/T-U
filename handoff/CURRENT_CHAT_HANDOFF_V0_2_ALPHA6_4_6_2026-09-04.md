## Alpha 6.4.6 scope

Alpha 6.4.5 completed real Tier 2/Tier 3 signatures for all 51 SVE/RSV NPCs that had previously been placeholders. Alpha 6.4.6 finishes the identity/art pass for those same 51 NPCs.

### Zero-sum per-character identity budget

Each completion NPC now has four authored axes:
- Power
- Reach
- Utility
- Tempo

Rules:
- Each axis is limited to `-2..+2`.
- The four axes must sum to exactly `0`.
- Every NPC has at least one positive axis and at least one negative axis.
- Therefore a strength must always be paid for by a weakness elsewhere.

Runtime envelope:
- Power: `+/- 3.5%` per point, max `+/- 7%`.
- Reach: `+/- 0.18 tile` per point, max `+/- 0.36 tile`.
- Utility: `+/- 6%` per point, max `+/- 12%` for control/knockback identity.
- Tempo: `18 ticks` cooldown shift per point, max `36 ticks` faster/slower.

The identity layer affects real expansion Signature runtime math and leaves the original role/class framework intact.

### Uniqueness gates

CI acceptance verified:
- `51/51 zero-sum identity budgets PASS`
- `51/51 unique semantic icon motif+variant pairs PASS`
- `51/51 unique effective gameplay fingerprints PASS`

A gameplay fingerprint includes:
- skill mechanic family

This blocks two completion NPCs from accidentally becoming exact gameplay clones.
