# Team Up Main Story, Codex Discovery, and Secret Rank Design

Status: DESIGN LOCK / NARRATIVE ROADMAP
Baseline branch: `v0.2-alpha6-7-23-capture-ceasefire-hardening`
Updated: 2026-09-10

## 1. Core theme

The title **Team Up** must be a thematic promise, not just a party-system name.

The whole story should ultimately prove one idea:

> **Some battles were never meant to be fought alone.**
>
> **Có những trận chiến vốn không dành cho một người.**

Awakening can make one person stronger. Team Up is what allows people with different strengths to accomplish something none of them can do alone.

The final conflict must not be solved by a single overpowered hero, including George. Even Rank S characters must need other people.

## 2. Opening trigger: the tenth kill

Team Up should not immediately dump a quest on the player when the mod is installed.

Instead, the mod silently counts valid normal monsters defeated after installation / story initialization.

Rules for the counter:

- Count normal, repeatable combat monsters.
- Do not count bosses.
- Do not count story/scripted/quest monsters.
- Do not count summons or mutation minions.
- Do not count Pelipper Pokémon protected by capture logic.
- Do not count explicit test-harness monsters.

On the **10th valid kill**, the next valid monster is forced into the first visible Mutation encounter instead of relying on the normal random Mutation roll.

This becomes the narrative trigger for The Surge.

The player should understand through gameplay that something has changed before any NPC explains it.

## 3. Main story spine

### Prologue: The Tenth Kill

The player's tenth valid kill triggers the first guaranteed Mutant.

The creature should appear to die, then visibly transform / revive into its Mutation state.

Story flag: **The Surge has begun.**

Mutation is initially presented as the threat, but later revealed to be a symptom of something deeper.

### Chapter I: Something Is Moving

Linus is the first observer.

He notices that wildlife and monsters are moving differently. His role is not to explain the system, but to tell the player that the Valley itself feels wrong.

Suggested thematic line:

> "Không phải chúng trở nên hung dữ hơn. Chúng đang chạy khỏi một thứ gì đó."

### Chapter II: The Adventurer

Marlon is the main combat mentor and the only character whose Rank S status is visible from the beginning.

When he sees evidence from the first Mutant, he recognizes something from decades ago but refuses to explain everything.

He hints at a forgotten incident:

- Adventurer's Guild arrived too late.
- A single miner had already contained the threat.
- That miner was never an official Guild member.
- Marlon does not reveal the name.

The legendary figure may be referred to as **The Last Blaster**.

The player should initially assume this was a dead or long-gone historical figure.

### Chapter III: Stronger Together

As The Surge grows, Team Up systems become increasingly important.

NPC combat recruitment should unlock progressively through story milestones rather than giving the player the full party immediately.

Suggested progression:

1. First Mutant / first investigation -> 1 NPC slot.
2. Early Guild investigation -> 2 NPC slots.
3. Discovery of the old mine connection -> 3 NPC slots.
4. Surge reaches HIGH / major chapter completion -> 4 NPC slots.
5. Final preparation -> full current party capacity.

This progression should reinforce that the Valley is responding to a growing threat by learning to fight together.

The current locked party rule remains: **5 people total including the Farmer**, which means single-player Farmer + up to 4 NPCs.

### Chapter IV: The Old Coal Mine

The current Surge is linked to an old coal-mine incident roughly thirty years earlier.

George's vanilla backstory must remain intact at the surface level:

- he worked in a coal mine;
- his leg became trapped;
- explosives detonated;
- the accident permanently impaired his mobility.

Team Up expands the untold context rather than replacing those facts.

The conspiracy layer:

- the mine company had continued operating in a dangerous sealed sector;
- a deep creature or entity was discovered beneath the coal seam;
- monster activity and early Mutation-like symptoms appeared around it;
- company / institutional records reduced the event to an industrial explosive accident;
- critical records were sealed or redacted.

George's "accident" occurred while deliberately collapsing the chamber to contain the creature.

His leg really was trapped. The explosive really did fall / detonate. The public story is therefore not false, only incomplete.

George did not kill the creature. He **buried it alive**.

Possible boss/source names remain placeholders until final lore naming:

- The Deep Heart
- Coalheart
- The Buried One

The entity is the deeper source or amplifier behind the modern Mutation phenomenon.

## 4. George: false Rank D -> Rank S Legendary

### Initial Codex presentation

George must not use `???` because that itself reveals a secret.

Before the story reveal, Codex should assess him exactly as an ordinary observer would:

- **Rank D**
- Combat Status: **Non-Combatant**
- No combat Signature
- No meaningful offensive ability
- Recruitment unavailable

Suggested system flavor:

> Advanced age. Permanent mobility impairment. Combat deployment not recommended.

Vietnamese:

> Tuổi cao. Khuyết tật vận động vĩnh viễn. Không khuyến nghị tham chiến.

If the player attempts recruitment, the scene should humorously underline the apparent absurdity:

> "Cậu/cô thật sự định mời một ông lão ngồi xe lăn đi đánh quái à?"

George should object to being patronized, but he still does not join at this point.

### Final reveal

The final boss should overpower the normal party even after the group reaches the battle prepared.

George appears as the hidden historical hero, still using his wheelchair. He must not be magically cured or made young.

Codex performs a **Combat Assessment Revised** update rather than revealing a pre-existing `???` field.

New profile:

- **Rank S · Legendary**
- Tank / Control / Demolition
- Title: **The Last Blaster**

Possible unique Rank S mechanic:

### THE LAST BLASTER

George can create boss weak points / break armor / stagger structures that ordinary party members cannot affect.

This is a rule-breaking mechanic, not merely higher damage.

George is still unable to defeat the final boss alone. His explosives create the opening that the rest of the party must exploit.

## 5. Evelyn: false low rank -> postgame Rank S

Evelyn should not reveal her real strength during the main story.

### Initial Codex presentation

Initial assessment should look completely ordinary:

- **Rank D** (or low C if later balance requires it; D is preferred for camouflage)
- Healer / Support
- weak but functional combat contribution
- weak Signature: **Garden Remedy**

Garden Remedy should provide modest healing with no spectacular combat effect. It should be usable, but weak enough that many players naturally prefer Harvey, Penny, Marnie, or another stronger support.

This is intentional misdirection.

### Hidden historical role

After George's old mine disaster, survivors suffered strange sickness / corruption resembling early Mutation exposure.

George got them out of the mine.

**Evelyn kept them alive afterward.**

She used gardening knowledge, local herbs, forest plants, and a rare plant associated with the contaminated mine region to stabilize affected miners.

She never considered this heroic and the Guild did not record her as a combatant.

### Postgame side story

Evelyn's Rank S reveal occurs only **after completion of the main Team Up story**.

Postgame symptoms begin appearing in survivors / participants / people exposed to residual corruption.

Harvey can treat symptoms but cannot identify the cause.

George recognizes what is happening and tells the player that the person they need is Evelyn.

Her side quest should not simply be another boss hunt.

Possible objectives:

- recover an old flower / herb strain;
- gather a forest ingredient during rain;
- retrieve a mine fungus / mineral sample;
- locate pages from an old treatment notebook;
- care for an affected NPC across several days;
- escort/protect Evelyn in a dangerous cave while she works.

During the final crisis of the side quest, Garden Remedy evolves through Awakening.

Codex revision:

- **Rank S**
- later optionally `S · Legendary` after full quest completion
- Title: **The Keeper**

### GRANDMOTHER'S GARDEN

Evelyn creates a large support zone with mechanics no ordinary healer has.

Potential effects:

- prevent downed allies from immediately leaving the fight;
- cleanse corruption / poison / major debuffs;
- heal multiple allies;
- allow limited revival of downed party members;
- scale in effectiveness with the number of allies still participating.

Crucial thematic rule:

**Grandmother's Garden should be significantly weaker when Evelyn is alone.**

Its strongest form exists only when there is a team to protect.

## 6. Marlon and other Rank S characters

### Marlon

Marlon is the deliberate exception to secret Rank rules.

His Codex profile should show:

- **Rank S · Legendary**

from the first time the player unlocks his entry.

This establishes Rank S as a real visible category and prevents players from assuming every strong character is hidden behind a fake rank.

Marlon acts as the early benchmark for what Rank S means.

He knows George's identity but respects an old promise and does not reveal it.

### MiMi

MiMi's source story already strongly establishes her exceptional nature.

Team Up should respect Cardcha progression and source ownership. Her S/Boss/Special status can follow her legitimate source-story availability rather than being artificially hidden by Team Up.

### Rank S design rule

Rank S must not mean simply:

- more HP;
- more damage;
- shorter cooldowns.

A Rank S character should **break one normal combat rule in a unique, lore-supported way**.

Examples:

- George: creates boss weak points / destroys armor ordinary characters cannot break.
- Evelyn: restores downed allies / creates team-scaling sanctuary mechanics.
- Marlon: future unique boss-pattern, parry, command, or veteran mechanic.
- MiMi: True Form / source-supported transformation behavior.

Rank A characters can be extremely strong. Rank S should feel mechanically surprising.

## 7. Codex discovery model

Codex must not be omniscient.

### Rule: meet first, then unlock

NPC entries should normally become visible only after the player meets that NPC for the first time.

Before first encounter:

- no full profile;
- optionally a hidden/silhouette slot if UI design later benefits from it, but no rank spoilers.

After first encounter:

- create Codex entry;
- show the current observed combat assessment.

### Observed rank, not true hidden power

Codex Rank is an **assessment based on what the player/world has observed so far**.

It is not a divine power scanner.

Therefore secret S characters use believable lower ranks before their story Awakening.

Do not use `???` as a generic secret-rank placeholder.

### Profile revision flow

Suggested state progression:

1. NPC not met -> no Codex entry.
2. First meeting -> observed profile unlocked.
3. Quest / Awakening progresses -> profile text can update.
4. True Awakening -> Combat Assessment Revised.
5. Major lore discovery -> new records / title / skill evolution unlocked.
6. Postgame revelation -> final profile state.

Possible Codex notice:

> **COMBAT ASSESSMENT REVISED**

This makes the Codex feel like a living record rather than a spoiler database.

## 8. Genuine Rank D characters

Secret S characters only work if many Rank D characters are genuinely Rank D.

The player must never learn a rule like "Rank D means hidden legendary".

Recommended genuine Rank D candidates:

### Pierre

- Rank D
- Damage / Support flavor
- weak, humorous but usable combat skill
- no hidden combat god reveal

Existing concept `Sales Pitch` can remain a small interrupt / debuff.

Pierre may receive a side quest and still remain Rank D. This is useful because it teaches that **questline != secret Awakening**.

### Lewis

- Rank D
- Support / Tank flavor
- may know important Valley history without personally being powerful
- small team-defense / positioning skill

Important principle: knowing lore does not grant combat rank.

### Elliott

- Rank D
- Support flavor
- low direct damage
- a morale / rhythm / resistance buff can make him situationally useful

Rank D must not mean unusable.

### Caroline

- Rank D
- Support / Healer
- Tea-based small healing / recovery effects
- no S-rank reveal planned

### Jodi

- Rank D
- Support
- safe civilian-style defensive support
- no secret legendary identity planned

### Gil

Gil is the ideal red herring.

Because he sits in the Adventurer's Guild beside Marlon, players may assume he must be a hidden Rank S.

He should remain genuinely low-ranked in the present day.

Possible explanation: he was once a capable adventurer, but age and retirement have reduced his present combat capability.

No magical restoration. No secret S reveal.

Possible line/theme:

> "Không phải huyền thoại nào cũng cần trở lại chiến trường."

This deliberately makes George's later reveal harder to predict.

## 9. Final boss and title payoff

The final battle must prove the title **Team Up**.

### Phase structure direction

1. Full party enters believing preparation is enough.
2. Boss reveals a stronger phase and begins defeating the group.
3. George arrives and reveals his Rank S identity.
4. George breaks the boss's protection / armor.
5. George still cannot finish the battle alone.
6. Other party members recover / re-enter through team mechanics.
7. Final phase requires multiple combat roles to create the finishing opportunity.
8. The finishing state is a **TEAM UP WINDOW**, not one hero's solo ultimate.

Possible required contributions:

- George / demolition -> exposes weak point;
- Tank -> controls boss pressure;
- Control -> manages adds / stagger window;
- Damage -> destroys exposed part;
- Support / Healer -> keeps formation alive;
- Farmer -> coordinates / triggers final action.

The exact combat implementation can vary, but the core rule is locked:

> **The final boss cannot be narratively solved by George alone, Marlon alone, Evelyn alone, or the Farmer alone.**

### Title moment

The phrase **TEAM UP** should be used sparingly in dialogue before the finale so it retains impact.

At the final synchronized attack / interaction, the UI can explicitly display:

# TEAM UP

This becomes the title payoff.

## 10. Ending statement

Suggested George reflection after the battle:

> "Tôi từng nghĩ việc khó nhất là việc một người đàn ông phải tự làm."

Evelyn may undercut him gently and keep the scene human.

The closing message should reinforce cooperation rather than individual hero worship.

Primary thematic line:

> **Some battles were never meant to be fought alone.**
>
> **Có những trận chiến vốn không dành cho một người.**

Secondary internal design statement:

> **Awakening makes one person stronger. Team Up makes the impossible possible.**
>
> **Awakening khiến một người mạnh hơn. Team Up khiến họ làm được điều không ai có thể làm một mình.**

## 11. Story and gameplay locks

The following are design locks for implementation:

- Do not spoil secret Rank S characters through `???` rank labels.
- Codex unlocks normally require first meeting the NPC.
- Codex displays observed assessments and can revise them later.
- George starts as genuine-looking Rank D / Non-Combatant with no combat Signature.
- George becomes S · Legendary only after the main-story reveal.
- Evelyn starts as low-ranked with deliberately modest healing.
- Evelyn's true S-rank Awakening is a postgame side-story reward.
- Marlon is visibly S · Legendary from the beginning.
- MiMi respects source-story progression and is not forcibly disguised by Team Up.
- Several genuine Rank D NPCs must remain Rank D permanently.
- A side quest does not automatically imply a rank upgrade.
- Rank D characters should still have niche/fun utility and should not be dead roster slots.
- Rank S characters receive unique rule-breaking mechanics rather than simple stat inflation.
- The final boss must require cooperation.
- George remains wheelchair-using throughout the story and combat; no magical cure.
- Team Up should expand George's mine accident context without contradicting the known public accident facts.
- Main story and postgame revelations should be written to preserve surprise on a first playthrough.
