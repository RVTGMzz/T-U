- Main Party NPC cap: 5 active NPCs. Farmers do not consume NPC slots.
- External combat companion cap: 2 active companions/summons/Pokemon across the shared farm.
- Vanilla main pet and ChaCha remain free unless a later design explicitly changes that.
- The compact dialogue shortcut label is generalized from `Pokemon` to `Companion` so it can cover Pokemon, pets, summons, and future creature providers.
