TEAM UP v0.2.0-alpha.6.6.14 - PELIPPER CAPTURE SAFETY SYNC

MUC TIEU CHINH
1. Khi Pelipper Town dung che do dung tan cong o nguong bat Pokemon (~10% HP), TOAN BO NPC Team Up ngung moi hanh dong gay damage vao Pokemon do.
2. Heal / revive / guard / support cho dong doi van tiep tuc trong combat context.
3. Neu con enemy khac, Team Up chuyen sang enemy khac thay vi dung ca tran.
4. Damage cua Team Up khong duoc vuot qua nguong capture trong mot hit lon.

TEST A - 1 POKEMON HOANG
- Dua Pokemon tu full HP xuong gan 10% bang Team Up.
- Quan sat khi cham nguong: Tank/DPS/Control/Support/Healer khong duoc danh tiep Pokemon do.
- Khong duoc co hit tre trong cung frame sau khi mot NPC vua dua HP xuong nguong.
- Pokemon phai con song va san sang cho flow capture cua Pelipper.

TEST B - DAMAGE CEILING
- Dung NPC damage cao vao Pokemon con khoang 11-15% HP.
- Hit cuoi cua Team Up phai bi clamp de khong day Pokemon xuong duoi nguong capture/KO.
- Khong crit vuot nguong tren target dang duoc capture-safety bao ve.

TEST C - HEAL / SUPPORT VAN CHAY
- Lam Farmer hoac 1 NPC mat mau.
- De Pokemon hoang o nguong capture la enemy duy nhat.
- Healer phai van heal / revive khi can.
- Tank guard va support cho phe minh duoc tiep tuc.
- Khong co offensive skill, AoE, stun/debuff cua Team Up cham vao Pokemon da protected.

TEST D - CO ENEMY KHAC
- De 1 Pokemon Pelipper o nguong capture + 1 monster khac con khoe.
- Team Up bo Pokemon protected va chuyen sang monster con lai.
- Sau khi monster kia het, team quay ve follow/support, khong quay lai danh Pokemon protected.

TEST E - SIGNATURE / AOE
- Abigail/Alex va cac NPC SVE/RSV co skill AoE.
- Pokemon Pelipper o sat enemy khac tai nguong capture khong duoc an damage AoE.
- Pokemon Pelipper tren nguong capture van co the bi danh, nhung damage phai bi clamp de khong vuot qua nguong.

TEST F - REGRESSION
- Single Pelipper target tren nguong capture van bi Tank/DPS/Control engage binh thuong.
- Shared companion quota van 2/2.
- Pokemon active khong flicker.
- NPC khong roi xuong nuoc; cau/song van muot.
- Curfew bye theo tinh cach van hoat dong.
- HP bar duoi chan / Switch equipment / Codex van binh thuong.

NEU CO LOI
- Gui SMAPI-latest.txt ngay sau tinh huong loi.
- Ghi HP Pokemon truoc/sau hit va ten NPC/skill vua danh.
