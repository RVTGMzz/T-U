A. PARTY VAULT - BLOCKER
1. Mo Kho chung Party.
2. Click trai lay item tu inventory nguoi choi, dat vao Vault.
3. Lay item tu Vault ve inventory.
4. Right-click chia stack o ca hai phia.
5. SHIFT + click item trong inventory: ca stack phai chuyen nhanh vao Vault neu con cho.
6. SHIFT + click item trong Vault: ca stack phai chuyen nhanh ve inventory neu con cho.
7. Controller: Y tren item dang chon = quick transfer sang phia con lai.
8. Nut Fill Stacks (icon rương vanilla): chi bo sung vao cac stack da co trong Vault, khong tao stack moi.
9. Nut Organize: gom stack + sap xep Vault, khong mat item.
10. Controller: cot phai cua inventory -> D-pad Right vao cac nut Fill Stacks / Organize / OK; A kich hoat.
11. B / ESC / OK dong menu duoc.
12. Neu dang cam item khi dong, item phai duoc tra an toan ve kho/inventory, khong mat do, khong ket menu.
13. Save/load roi mo lai Vault, item van con.
