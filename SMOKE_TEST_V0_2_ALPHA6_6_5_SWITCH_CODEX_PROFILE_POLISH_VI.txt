A. EQUIPMENT - SWITCH CONTROLLER
--------------------------------
1. Mo menu cua 1 NPC dang trong party > Trang bi.
2. Dung D-pad/left stick dua focus sang tui do.
3. Chon 1 vu khi hop le.
4. Nhan nut Action dung de tuong tac tren Switch DUNG 1 LAN.
   PASS:
   - item roi khoi tui;
   - slot Vu khi NPC hien item that;
   - chi co 1 thong bao trang bi;
   - khong can double-click;
   - khong xuat hien trang bi roi thao ngay sau do.
5. Thu lai voi giay va ring/trinket.
6. Dung right stick dua controller cursor len 1 item khac, nhan Action 1 lan.
   PASS: dung item duoi pointer duoc xu ly.
7. Spam/giu ngan nut Action.
   PASS: debounce van ngan duplicate transaction.
8. Mouse double-click item trong tui van equip trong cua so 450ms.
9. X / nut Thao trang bi van unequip binh thuong.

B. CODEX - KHONG SKIP HO SO
----------------------------
1. Mo danh sach Codex co it nhat 5 NPC A-B-C-D-E.
2. Dung D-pad Down tung lan.
   PASS: A -> B -> C -> D -> E, khong bo qua dong.
3. Dung left stick Down tung nhip ngan.
   PASS: cung chi di 1 ho so moi input, khong A -> C.
4. Thu Up tu E ve A.
   PASS: moi input chi lui 1 ho so.
5. Dropdown Role/Status/Source van di 1 option moi lan.
6. Right-stick / mouse wheel scroll van dong bo viewport va selection, khong snap nguoc.
