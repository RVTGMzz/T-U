TEAM UP v0.2.0-alpha.6.6.12 - RELATIONSHIP CURFEW + CONTEXT HEALTH

MUC TIEU
- NPC khong con di theo va ngu gat qua khuya mot cach ky la.
- Gio ve nha phu thuoc do than thiet voi Farmer tuyen NPC.
- Thanh mau nam ngay duoi chan NPC, khong con HUD cot ben trai.
- Thanh mau chi xuat hien theo ngu canh de man hinh sach hon.

1) CURFEW / GIO VE NHA
- 0-2 tim: toi 23:00 NPC phai roi Following/Waiting va tro ve schedule goc.
- 3-5 tim: toi 00:00.
- 6-7 tim: toi 01:00.
- 8-9 tim: toi 02:00.
- 10+ tim hoac spouse: Team Up cho phep toi 03:00 neu modpack cho phep ngay keo dai toi do.
- Team Up KHONG thay doi hard sleep clock cua Stardew. Neu game bat ngu som hon thi NPC se theo toi luc game ket thuc ngay.
- Roster/progression/equipment phai con nguyen. NPC chi chuyen Inactive cho phan con lai cua ngay.
- Sang ngay moi co the goi/tuyen lai theo flow hien tai.

2) NPC-LINKED COMPANION
- Neu NPC co Pokemon/summon lien ket, khi NPC het gio dong hanh thi companion do chuyen Standby.
- Khong duoc de NPC ve nha ma Pokemon cua NPC van bi Team Up giu trong formation.
- Khong duoc tai xuat hien flicker Pelipper.

3) HEALTH BAR DUOI CHAN
- Khong con danh sach ten + thanh mau doc ben trai man hinh.
- Thanh mau phai nam sat duoi chan NPC.
- Ngoai combat, full HP, khong noi chuyen: thanh mau an.
- Khi NPC bi gay sat thuong: thanh mau hien va giu khoang 3 giay.
- Khi con dang combat: thanh mau tiep tuc hien.
- Sau khi combat ket thuc: giu them khoang 3 giay roi an.
- Khi dang noi chuyen truc tiep voi NPC: thanh mau cua NPC do hien.
- NPC downed: thanh mau van hien de de nhan biet.

4) REGRESSION
- Pelipper Pokemon Farmer + NPC: khong flicker, Team Up khong giat movement authority.
- Pokemon/summon: khong co Ho so.
- Song/cau: van muot, NPC nguoi khong roi xuong nuoc.
- Switch equip/unequip van dung.
- Codex di tung ho so mot, khong skip.
- People cap 6 total va combat companion cap 2 giu nguyen.
